A SMALL FIELD GUIDE

Made of stardust.

VOID ATLAS is a real-time, procedural artwork: three imagined encounters with matter, gravity, and light. Shapes are generated in code. No photographs, paid assets, or remote services.

This is an artistic interpretation, not an astrophysical simulation. Three.js powers the WebGL edition; a lighter Canvas edition keeps the experience available.
